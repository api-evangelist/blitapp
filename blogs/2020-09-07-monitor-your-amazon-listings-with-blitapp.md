Capture, Review and Archive your Amazon listings Many users subscribe to Blitapp to monitor their Amazon listings: product page, search results, reviews, special deals, etc. Amazon tends to make unannounced and unwanted changes to listings. Our users want to quickly see when a change happens and be able to prove what their product page or search results looked like before the change.
