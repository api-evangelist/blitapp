Sometimes, pages display banners or overlays—such as cookie banners, subscription popups, etc.—that hide the pages’ main content. With Blitapp, you can hide these banners and overlays before taking a screenshot of the page. We’ll go through a couple of examples to explain how to find the element to hide or click on and add it to your capture settings.
