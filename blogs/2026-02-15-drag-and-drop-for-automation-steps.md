We just added drag-and-drop support for Automation Steps and Initial Automation Steps. You can now reorder your steps directly in the editor, without deleting and recreating them. Why this matters Automation workflows often evolve. You might need to: move a sleep step before a click. run a navigate step earlier in the sequence place a screenshot step after a specific interaction reorganize loop...
