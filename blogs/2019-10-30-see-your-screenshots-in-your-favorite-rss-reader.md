We have added a new App: RSS. Unlike the other Apps , it does not store your screenshots on your own cloud but offers an alternative to e-mail to visualize and share your web captures. To start generating your RSS feed(s), follow these steps: Create your RSS App(s) Use the App(s) Add the feed to your favorite RSS reader Create an App The left menu has a new entry for Apps .
