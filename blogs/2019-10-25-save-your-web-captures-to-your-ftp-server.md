You can now get your screenshots uploaded directly to your cloud storage through Apps , including your own FTP server. To start getting your captures uploaded to your FTP server, follow these steps: Configure your FTP server Configure your App Use the App Configure your FTP server Make sure your FTP server can be accessed from the Internet. We recommend that you create a separate user for Blitapp.
