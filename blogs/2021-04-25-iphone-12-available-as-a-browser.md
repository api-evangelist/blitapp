We have added the iPhone 12 as a browser and the existing iPhone 5. We also made several improvements to both mobile browsers, including better support for mobile-only Javascript API. The iPhone 12 has a native resolution of 320px by 480px, the iPhone 12 is 390px by 844px. The iPhone 12 also supports the dark mode we introduced earlier.
