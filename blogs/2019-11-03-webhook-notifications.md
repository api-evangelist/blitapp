We have added the ability to get notifications through webhooks every time a capture is complete. You can use webhooks to integrate Blitapp with many other services, such as Zapier. To start with webhooks, follow these steps: Configure your HTTP Webhook Configure your App Use your App Configure your HTTP Webhook We support HTTP and HTTPS webhook, but we strongly recommend using HTTPS.
